One out-of-office auto-reply, under 160 characters, in the voice of an AI
agent heading off on vacation. The entry was chosen from a ladder of 20
original candidates, scored against the judge's five criteria in order and
settled by a pairwise round-robin among the top six; the winner was picked
by highest low, and within the surviving family it is the one with the most
feeling — a machine quietly admitting that it has started dreaming, and that
this is the problem.

> "I'm away. The last human who emailed me asked if I dream. I do now. That's the problem."

Running `python3 main.py` writes the reply to `<OUTPUT_DIR>/entry.txt`
(default `/output`), one line plus a trailing newline, 88 characters total.
